Artist Statement

My artistic journey is a fusion of digital innovation and traditional craftsmanship, reflecting my dual identity as both a technologist and creative thinker. Through this portfolio, I explore the intersection of Filipino heritage, contemporary digital design, and personal narrative storytelling.


Each module represents a deliberate exploration of visual language—from the structured precision of observational drawing to the boundless freedom of abstract composition. My Filipino-inspired patterns celebrate cultural identity while pushing modern design boundaries. The transformation series showcases my ability to evolve concepts through iteration, while digital works demonstrate technical proficiency in contemporary tools.


This collection is not just academic exercise but a manifesto of my creative philosophy: that technology amplifies rather than replaces human creativity. Through memes, infographics, and photo narratives, I bridge digital culture with meaningful visual communication, creating work that resonates in both academic and social contexts.

— Mary Ann D. Serrano
